Sysdyne
Illustrations

A series of 15 animated machine illustrations made for Sysdyne Technologies, a maker of control software for concrete batch plants. The illustrations show the plant's equipment: mixers, conveyors, silos, pumps and a mixer truck. They are meant to show the operator in the interface what is happening at the plant. Each machine is a vector SVG illustration animated with JavaScript. The motion follows how the equipment works, and it can be stopped, resumed or reversed.
